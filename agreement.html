Parties

Agency: GrowTech Media | @growtechmedia

Creator (Talent): Details provided in the signing section below.

Purpose

This is an ongoing retainer agreement. It does not specify any individual campaign, brand, or posting schedule — those details are shared separately via a Campaign Brief for each campaign.
  • The Creator is available to GrowTech Media as an on-call talent partner
  • Both parties commit to a minimum engagement period of 3 months
  • Campaign frequency, payment, and brand details are determined per campaign

What This Covers

  • Creator's availability and willingness to work with GrowTech Media
  • General conduct and professionalism standards
  • Usage rights framework for all campaigns under this retainer
  • Notice period and exit terms

Does NOT cover:

  • Specific campaign dates, brands, or deliverables (per campaign)
  • Fixed monthly payment (payment is per reel, per campaign)
  • Guaranteed number of campaigns per month

How Campaigns Work

  1. GrowTech Media sends Creator a Campaign Brief (shoot date, brand, rate)
  2. Creator confirms availability within 24 hours
  3. A single Campaign Agreement is signed for that specific campaign
  4. GrowTech Media handles all scripting, shooting, editing, post-production
  5. Creator shows up on set, follows direction, and posts the final reel
Declining more than 3 campaigns in a month without valid reason may be considered a breach of good faith.

Creator's Commitments

Availability & Communication

  • Respond to GrowTech Media within 12 hours on working days
  • Keep Instagram account active throughout the retainer
  • Inform GrowTech Media if follower count or niche significantly changes

Brand & Content Standards

  • Maintain a brand-safe Instagram — no offensive, hateful, or controversial content
  • Not work as direct talent for a competing influencer marketing agency without informing GrowTech Media
  • Not independently approach GrowTech Media's clients for 6 months after retainer ends

Exit & Notice Policy

Creator wants to leave:

  • Minimum 10 days' written notice via WhatsApp or email
  • All confirmed campaigns must be completed before exit
  • Leaving before 3-month minimum without valid reason: advance on pending campaigns forfeited

Valid reasons for early exit without penalty:

  • Medical emergency (documentation required)
  • Relocation outside India
  • Mutual written agreement

Usage Rights

Non-exclusive, royalty-free rights for all reel content across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, Meta/Google ads, WhatsApp, website, email. Duration: 12 months per reel. Territory: India.

Non-Solicitation & Confidentiality

Creator agrees not to approach GrowTech Media's clients for 6 months after retainer ends. All client identities, campaign strategies, payment rates, and internal workflows are strictly confidential.
